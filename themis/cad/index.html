What this does not prove

  • Thrust is estimated, not measured: 3.6–5.0:1 assumes 650–900 g per motor on 4S. Bench-test one motor, prop and ESC before buying the set.
  • Lines marked Estimate need checking on delivery. They include the lens, the camera board's weight and hole pattern, the UWB module's weight and the capacitor size.
  • The UWB module is modelled as a bare DWM3000 on a carrier PCB that is not designed yet. The Makerfabs ESP32 UWB DW3000 board would remove that PCB, but at 72 × 32.6 mm it needs a new mount.
  • Pi port heights come from the Pi 4. The PM02's size comes from a retailer, not Holybro.
  • Structural strength, vibration, thermals in the pod, and cable bend radii are not analysed.
